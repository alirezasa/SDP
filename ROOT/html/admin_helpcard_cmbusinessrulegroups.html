On this page, you can create and manage business rules to automate custom actions such as updating custom module fields or execute scripts on all custom module records that satisfy specific criteria.

    Categorize and execute business rules in different rule groups. To create a new group, click Rule Group and provide the required details.

    Add a new business rule to the group by hovering over the group and clicking Add Rule.

  • Select whether the rule must be executed - when the record is created, edited, moved to trash, or deleted permanently.
  • Specify the conditions to be met.
  • If the rule is set to execute when the record is edited and based on conditions, you can choose to execute the rule:
    • Every time the record is edited.
    • Only when the fields configured in the conditions are edited.
  • Choose the action to be executed on records via the business rule - field update, custom function, or execute scripts.
    • Choosing Deleted Permanently with abort option retains the record in trash when deleted. Field update is not supported for this option. However, you can configure custom scripts/functions to abort the delete operation on trashed records.

    List View Actions

    From the list view of business rules, you can organize business rules and rule groups, configure cascade execution, and move rules across groups.
