Milestone Templates

Milestones are achievable targets points within a project. Almost like projects within a bigger project.

Title: Provide an appropriate title.

Description: A brief account of why this milestone is important.

Priority: Choose from low, medium, normal, or high. You can also configure custom priorities on the Admin tab.

Status: Choose from open, closed, onhold, or canceled. You can also configure custom statuses on the Admin tab.

Estimated hours: Provide a rough estimate of the time needed to complete this milestone.

Save: The drop-up menu on this button allows you to also add a milestone task or a new milestone.
