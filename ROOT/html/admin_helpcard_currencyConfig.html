Currency

You can configure different currencies that your company uses for its vendor transactions. The base currency is the local currency of your company.

To add a new currency,

  1. Click New.

  2. Provide the following information:

    • Currency Name: Select your preferred currency from the drop-down list.

    • Currency Symbol: Provide your preferred symbol for the currency.

    • Exchange Rate: Provide the exchange rate with respect to your base currency.

  3. Finally, click Save.

Edit or Delete Currencies

  • To edit a currency , click the name of the currency or click the Actions icon beside the name and choose Edit, make necessary changes, and click Update.

  • To delete currencies, select the currencies, and click the Delete icon. To delete a single currency, click the Actions icon beside the currency name and choose Delete.

Note

  • The exchange rate is not applicable for the default base currency and you can only edit its Symbol.

  • The default base currency cannot be deleted.
