Products

Products refer to the commodities purchased by your organization. For instance, the workstation “Latitude D600” is a product of the "Workstation" product type.

You can create products and configure depreciation using different methods as required.

Add New Product
  1. Click New.
  2. Provide the following information:
    • Product Type: Select the product type under which the product should be classified.
    • Name: Provide a name that uniquely identifies the product. E.g., Dell Latitude D600.
    • Manufacturer: Enter the manufacturer name.
    • Part No: Enter the part number of the product.
    • Cost: Specify the cost of purchasing the product.
  3. Configure the depreciation details for the product:
    • Depreciation Method: Select from Declining Balance, Double Declining Balance, Straight Line, or Sum of the Years Digit.
    • Calculation Factor: Choose Useful Life or Decline Percent. (Available only for Declining Balance and Straight Line methods.)
    • Useful Life (in months) / Decline Percent: Provide a value as applicable.
    • Salvage Value ($): Enter the book value of the asset after all depreciation has been fully expensed.
  4. Click Add Image to upload product images.
  5. Provide a relevant description about the product.
  6. Click Save or Save and Add New.
Configure Depreciation in Bulk
  1. Select the products in the list view.
  2. Click Configure Depreciation.
  3. Provide the necessary details.
  4. Click Save.
List View Operations
  • Click the Actions icon beside a product name to edit, delete, or configure depreciation.
  • To configure depreciation in bulk, select the products on the list view and click the Configure Depreciation button on the toolbar.
  • To bulk-delete, select the products on the list view and click the Delete icon on the toolbar.
  • Use the Search icon to search through the columns listed.
  • Use the Column Chooser icon to customize the columns listed.
Associate Vendor
  1. Click the Expand icon beside the name of your preferred product.
  2. Click Associate Vendor and provide the following information:
    • Vendor: Select a vendor from the drop-down list.
    • Price: Enter the price of the product.
    • Tax Rate: Provide the applicable tax rate.
    • Warranty Period: Configure the warranty period in years and months.
    • Maintenance Vendor: Select a maintenance vendor for the product.
    • Comments: Add any additional information.
  3. Click Save.
Edit or Delete Product-Vendor Associations
  • Click the Actions icon beside a vendor name to edit or delete a product-vendor association.
  • To bulk-delete product-vendor associations, select the associations and click the Delete icon.

Note: You can also associate products to a vendor under Admin > Customization > Asset Management > Vendors.
