Facility Service Templates

Create and save templates of frequently created facility services such as cab services, ATM services, etc., to aid users while they create similar facility services.

On this page, you can create a new facility service template, edit or delete the existing ones.

To create a new facility service template, click New Facility Service Template.

The new facility service template has two sections,

1. Details: Here, you can define the facility service template by adding a display name, name, and description of it.

2. Fields: Once you have saved the details section, you can add new fields to the facility service template by dragging the required fields from the New Field Section on the right side.

To name a section, click Untitled text and type in the desired section name.

Finally, save the field configuration.
