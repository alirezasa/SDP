Service Categories enables you to enter all the IT Services offered in your organization. The IT Services are essential to infer the impact caused by a request.  

Add New Service

Click the New Service link. The Add IT Service form is displayed.
Specify the Name of the IT Service in the given text field. This field is mandatory.
Specify the IT Service details in the Description field.
Click Save. If you do not want to add an IT Service, click Cancel.
