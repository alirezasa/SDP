About Zia:

Zia helps users perform 20 different operations, including raising incident/service requests, assigning technicians to requests, accessing service catalogs, and viewing solutions/announcements. Users can perform all these operations by typing a command or by asking the Zia Bot.

On this page, you can enable or disable the Zia Bot. Once enabled, the Zia Bot icon will be displayed at the right bottom bar for users and will show actions only as per the users' scope. When you enable the auto-open option, the Zia Bot will open automatically each time a requester logs in.


On this page, you can:

  • Enable/disable the Zia Bot Widget.
  • Embed Zia Bot to external websites so that users can access Zia directly from their websites.
  • Track who enabled Zia and when (Timestamp) under View History

After you enable Zia, paste the link on the external website. The user can also switch to the other help desks (they have access to) by selecting the help desk from the Zia widget drop-down.
