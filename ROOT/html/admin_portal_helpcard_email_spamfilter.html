Spam Filter

Define filter criteria to block any unnecessary or spam messages from entering your inbox. Mails that fulfill the criteria you have defined are dropped, and no requests are created from them.
Configure the Spam Filter.

To mark out all spam messages, ensure that you define the rule clearly with choose if the rule must match ALL conditions or any one of the specified conditions.
Under Define rule, select the criteria from the drop-down and select the condition. Click the Choose button to add comma separated entries to the filter conditions. Say, the Subject field can contain Out of office or Not in today.
Click Add to Rules and Save.
