ServiceDesk Plus Integration with WhatsApp

Set up ServiceDesk Plus integration with WhatsApp to enable seamless communication between your ServiceDesk Plus instance and users through WhatsApp. This integration allows users to create a request, perform approval actions, interact with help desk technicians, receive notifications, and access support directly from their WhatsApp accounts.


Setup Integration

  • Enter the Phone Number ID associated with your WhatsApp Business Account.
  • Provide the Business Phone Number linked to your WhatsApp account.
  • Enter the Access Token generated for your WhatsApp Business API.
  • Provide the App Secret from your WhatsApp application settings.
  • Under SDP Hosted Domain, the alias URL configured for your application will be auto-populated if it is a public URL hosted over HTTPS. If your application is hosted elsewhere, specify the publicly hosted SDP domain.

After you submit the configurations, a new tab called Feature Configurations will appear. In this tab, you can manage various settings related to WhatsApp integration.


Feature Configurations

  • Use the Enable/Disable WhatsApp Integration toggle button to activate or deactivate the WhatsApp integration.
  • Toggle the Bot Commands to enable or disable bot commands within the WhatsApp integration.
  • Toggle the Request Notifications to control whether notifications are sent to users via WhatsApp.
  • Toggle the Chat with Technician option to allow or prevent users from chatting with technicians through WhatsApp.
  • Toggle the Authentication via Email OTP to enable or disable user authentication using an email OTP instead of logging in with credentials.
