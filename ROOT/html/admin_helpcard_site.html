Multiple Site Model

If your organization has various regional offices across the globe, then these offices can be configured as Sites. The purpose of configuring Sites is to maintain a single installation of this application irrespective of the location, working hours and IT operations executed in the sites. A Site's configuration can also be made to refer another site's Business Rules, Operational Hours, Holidays and SLA by selecting the appropriate settings.

By configuring Sites, you can restrict technicians from viewing requests, problems, changes and assets of certain sites, organize your IT operations across sites and enable prompt handling of requests raised from different sites.

When you get started with configurations, the Operational Hours, Holidays, Departments, Business Rules and Service Level Agreements configured is considered as the Default Settings. So while adding sites, you have options to either select the refer settings, or configure separate settings for the site.

Example:

The options – Refer Settings and Custom Settings – can be explained better with an example.

Let’s say your head office is in New York, USA and you also have offices in Boston, Washington and Tokyo. The IT operations in the USA region are handled by technicians located in Boston. And the IT operations in Japan are handled by technicians in Tokyo.

Assume the office in Tokyo works 6 days a week, while the office in USA region works 5 days a week. The Departments, Business Rules and Service Level Agreements are similar for all the sites. And, you have configured the default settings to suit the USA region.

In the application, the offices in New York, Boston, Washington and Tokyo are configured as sites. Since the default settings suit the USA region, you can Refer the Default Settings for the sites – New York, Boston and Washington. When you refer a default setting, the site will not have separate site based configurations i.e., these sites will not be listed in the drop down menu while viewing the settings.

Since the site Tokyo works 6 days a week, you need to customize the operational hours for the site by selecting Custom Settings beside operational hours. The site will be listed in Operational Hours for drop-down box and you can customize the operational hours for the site. 

NOTE: Technicians with administrator privilege can add, edit and delete sites. The Site Admin has the privilege to view and edit his/her site details. They cannot add nor delete sites.

Adding Sites

To add a new Site, click Add New Site link. The Add Site form has 4 blocks namely, Organization Details, Address, Contact Information and Related Settings.

  • Organization Details: Enter the Site Name and brief information about the IT operations in the Site in Description field. The Site Name is a mandatory field. Select the Region in which the site is located and the Time Zone of the site.
  • Address: Specify the Address of the site, the City where the branch is located, the Postal Code and State. Select the Country where the site is located from the drop-down.
  • Contact Information: Specify the Contact Information of the site such as the E-mail ID, Phone No., Fax No. and Web URL.

Active and inactive sites

  • Active and inactive sites can now be displayed in separate views.
  • To delete a site that is no longer used in any module, select it and then click Delete. The deleted site will immediately become inactive.
  • To revert an inactive site to the active mode, select the site and click Mark as Active.

Actions

Delete Site(s): To delete one or more sites, select that particular site and click 'Delete Site(s)' from Actions. Use 'Select All' incase you want to delete all the sites.

Change Site Association: If the Operational Hours, Holidays, Technicians, Groups, Rules and SLAs of the Site to be deleted is being referred by one or more Site(s), then the deletion process will consist of two steps:

Step 1: In the first step, entities like Assets, Incident Request Templates, PM tasks and Requesters associated with that particular Site to be deleted will be listed. Since you require the selected site to be deleted, you need to move those associated entities of the sites that refer the site to be deleted to refer either to the default settings or the settings of sites listed. If that particular Site is not associated to any Site, then click on 'Finish'.

Step 2: In step two, Site(s) to be deleted will be listed along with the settings that they refer (Eg: Operational hours, Holidays etc.,). Change the Site's references and click 'Finish'. You can either choose the refer Site's settings to refer default settings or any other Site's settings that you require.

Related Settings: Select the configurations for the site. You can either choose Refer Settings, or Custom Settings to modify the preset configuration.

Refer Settings: The site follows the configured settings of another site that you choose in the application.  If Default settings is being referred by a site, then, any change made in the default settings will be reflected in the refer site as well.
Let’s take the same example of the 3 offices in the USA region. Since the default settings are configured to suit the USA region, you just need to refer the default settings to the 3 sites.

Custom Settings: You need to customize the configurations for the site.
Taking the above example about the office in Tokyo that operates 6 days a week unlike the default operational hours that operates 5 days a week. While adding the site, select Custom Settings beside Operational Hours, after which, you can configure the working days for the site –Tokyo in Operational Hours. 

Click Save. Click Save and Add New button to save and add another site. Click Cancel if you do not want to add the site.
