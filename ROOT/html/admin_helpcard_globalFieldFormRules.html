Field and Form Rules

On this page, you can configure field and form rules and associate them with all or specific templates.

Define actions to be executed when:

1. The form is loaded

2. A field is modified

3. The request is submitted


Field and form rules are executed on the client side.


To Add a New Rule

Click Create New Rule and provide the required details.

Click Save to add the rule.

Use the icons beside a rule to edit, delete, and enable/disable it.

Click the Actions drop-down to edit, delete, enable, and disable the rules in bulk.
