You can configure two types of survey—request-based and general surveys. You can further select a request-based survey to be an incident survey, service survey, or incident and service survey.

To Configure a Survey

  • Click Add to select a survey type.
  • Provide a unique name to your survey and enter questions under Workspace.
  • In request-based surveys, configure survey frequency and set matching conditions for requests to send the survey.
  • In general surveys, choose whether to survey periodically and if yes, enter the survey frequency. Then, select the target audience from the available groups and specify a count limit in percentage, if required.
  • Click Save.

Edit the survey and go to Translation >> New Translation.

Select a language, enter the translated equivalent of the survey questions, and click Save.

Note: You must possess the multi-lingual license to configure translation.

Enter a note or any extra information about the survey under Comments. This is for your reference only, users will not be able to view it.

You can view the survey results under Reports.

You can view all the survey logs in History.

 

To Initiate a Survey
 Click Initiate Survey button or select the same from Settings menu. You can also click Publish Survey inside the survey.

To Reorder Request-based Surveys
When you have published multiple request-based surveys, use the Reorder button to sequence the surveys in order of decreasing priority.

To Suspend a Survey
Click Stop Survey. Users cannot take a suspended survey. You can initiate a suspended survey anytime.

To Inactivate a Survey
To terminate a published survey and simultaneously save the survey's user responses, you can mark the survey as inactive.
Select Mark as Inactive from the Settings menu next to the survey.

To Delete a Survey
You can delete only unpublished surveys and published surveys with zero responses.
Click Delete Survey from the Settings menu or inside the survey.
As for the surveys with responses, you can mark them inactive. 

Survey Templates
Save the commonly used surveys as templates that will help you save time and effort when you configure new surveys. You can simply import a template into new surveys.
To save a survey as a template, click Save as Template on the footer of the survey form.
Note that the template will be saved in the survey name. To edit and then save the survey as a new template, you must provide a different name to the survey or delete the existing template.
To import a template into a new survey, click Survey Templates on the Workspace canvas area.
Then, select a template and click Import.
If you want to add new questions to a survey in addition to the template questions, remember to import the template first and then add new questions.
