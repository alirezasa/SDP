Departments specific to each site can be configured here along with the Department Heads and other department roles.

Benefits of configuring departments

  • Helps in segregating the issues by department and provides an option for the other users / the department heads to view all the issues that are raised in their department.
  • Helps in generating department based reports.


Benefits of configuring department heads

  • Helps in easier selection of approval authority (department heads) for service request / incident requests.
  • Helps in better automation using business rules.


To add a new department, select a site from the department for drop-down, this will make sure the department will come under that site. Click Add New Department, and fill out the displayed form.

Add Department Heads, Attributes, and Org Roles pertaining to each department.

Note: If there are no sites configured then the department for drop-down will not be displayed.

All these operations, including deletion and update, can be done only in the ESM directory.

Note: Business Impact of a department can be modified under individual service desk instance.
