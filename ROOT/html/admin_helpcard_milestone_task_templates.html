Task under Milestone Templates

Tasks within a project help break down the entire project into smaller, doable chunks of activity that are easy to track and monitor.

Use Task Template: Choose from the already available templates.

Title: Provide an appropriate title.

Status: Choose from open, onhold, closed, or resolved. You can also configure custom statuses on the Admin tab.

Description: Describe the importance of the task.

Estimated Effort: Provide the estimated time required to complete the task.

Priority: Choose from low, normal, medium, or high. You can also configure custom priorities on the Admin tab.

Additional Cost($): Add an estimate of how much this task will cost.

Associated Milestone: You can leave this empty, if you do not want to associate the task to any milestone.

Task Type: Choose from the task types list.

Comments: Add any extra information or details about the task.
