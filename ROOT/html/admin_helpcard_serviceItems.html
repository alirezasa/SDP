Under Service Catalog, you can list all the IT services that are available for your users. You can group these services under categories such as Application, Communication, Email, Hardware, and so on. You can also configure new categories, besides the already available ones, and add IT services.

To add a new category, click the New Category drop-down, select the service, and fill out the displayed form.

To modify the display of the categories on this page, click Customize Portal Layout.
