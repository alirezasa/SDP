Vendor Services

Configure your day-to-day services that you can purchase from different vendors. You can associate vendor services with specific vendors.

Add New Vendor Service

    To add a new vendor Service,

  1. Click New.

  2. Provide the following information:

    • Service Name: Enter your vendor Service.

    • Currency: Select your preferred service type. You can also add a new service type. To do this, click the Add icon beside the field, provide a name and description for the service type, and click Save.

    • GL Code: Select the GL Code for the service type.

    • Part No: Provide a part number for the service type.

    • Description: Provide a relevant description.

  3. Finally, click Save.

Note:

  • You can edit or delete only the custom service types. To do this, hover over the service type on the drop-down list and click the appropriate option.

  • The default service types cannot be edited or deleted.

Edit or Delete Products

  • To edit a vendor service, click the name of the vendor service or click the Actions icon beside the name and choose Edit, make necessary changes, and clickUpdate.

  • To delete vendor services, select the vendor services, and click the Delete icon. To delete a single vendor service, click the Actions icon beside the service name and choose Delete.

Note:

  • When a service is deleted, its vendor relationships will automatically become void.

  • You can also add services to vendors under Admin >> Asset Management >> Vendors.

  • When deleting vendor services that are associated with purchase orders, they will be marked as inactive and cannot be used with future purchases. You can view inactive vendor services by choosing Inactive Vendor Services from the view filter drop-down on the top-left. To restore, select the vendor services, and click Mark as Active.

Associating vendors

To associate a vendor with a service,,

  1. Click the Expand icon beside the name of your preferred service.

  2. Click Associate Vendor and provide the following information:

    1. Vendor: Select a vendor from the drop-down list with whom you want to associate the service.

    2. Cost: Enter the cost for service.

    3. Tax rate: Provide the applicable tax rate.

    4. Service Period:Configure the applicable warranty period in years and months.

    5. Support vendor:Select a support vendor for the service.

    6. Comments:Add any additional information.

  3. Finally, click Save.

Edit or DeleteService-Vendor Associations

  • To edit a service-vendor association, click the name of the vendor or click the Actionsicon beside the name and choose Edit,make necessary changes, and clickUpdate.

  • To delete service-vendor associations, select the associations, and click the Delete icon. To delete a single association, click the Actions icon beside the vendor's name and choose Delete.
