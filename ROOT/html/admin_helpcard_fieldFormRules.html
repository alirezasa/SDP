Field and Form Rules

Under this tab, you can add, disable, enable, and delete field and form rules.

Define actions that must execute on the request when:

1. The form is loaded (on form-load).

2. Form fields are changed.

3. The request is submitted.


Field and form rules are executed on the client side.


Click Create New Rule, fill out the displayed form, and click Save.

To see how the field and form rule will be executed on a request, preview the template.
