Provide the complete information about your organization here.

Name: Enter the full name of your organization here. This will be used as a header which generating purchase orders.
Description: Provide a brief information about what your organization does or any other details that you feel is relevant.
E-mail ID: Enter the general contact e-mail ID for your organization.

For the remaining fields, Address, City, Postal Code, State, Country, Phone Number, Fax No., and Web URL, specify the relevant information. These details will be used in various modules.

You can import the company logo(s) and use any one of them in places the organization details are being used.

  1. Click Import Image button beside the Company Logo field.
  2. Click the Browse button and choose the image file from the file chooser window and click Open.
  3. Click Import.
By default, the Use this image check box is enabled. If you do not wish to use this image, then disable the check box.

Click Save to save the settings.

An organization can have its branches in various regions and sites, based on which the operational hours, holidays, SLA and business rules are calculated. Say for ex, the organization ZOHO has its branch at Chennai and Mumbai in India and Pleasanton in USA. The attributes such as operational hours, holidays, SLA and Business rules are calculated accordingly, for the corresponding sites.
