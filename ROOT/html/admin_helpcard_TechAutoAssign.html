Technicians can be automatically assigned to requests using the Technician Auto Assign option. The Technician Auto Assign follows the Round Robin or Load Balancing model, and based on the availability of the technicians, the requests are assigned. So, if a technician is unavailable on the specified due by date, requests are not assigned to that technician.

Note: For site specific requests, technicians associated to a site are auto assigned to requests raised from that site alone. Similarly, for requests assigned to particular group, the technicians associated to the group are assigned to the requests.

  1. Select Enable Technician Auto Assign check box. This check box should be enabled to select the consecutive options.  

  2. Requests are assigned to the technicians either through Round Robin or Load Balancing model.

    Load Balancing:     The number of requests that are In progress and assigned to a technician is taken as the load of the technician. So on selecting this model, technicians with the least load are assigned to requests.
    Round Robin:     In this model, technicians are assigned to the request irrespective of the load.

  3. The Technician Auto Assign option can be executed for newly created requests, edited requests or, to both created and edited requests. Select your option by enabling the radio button.  

  4. On creating a new request, the Technician Auto Assign can be applied to the Unassigned Requests or to the assigned/unassigned Requests.
    Unassigned Requests:     On selecting this option, the Technician Auto Assign is applied to requests that are unassigned even after passing through all the Business Rules and Category selections.
    All Requests:     When this option is selected, the technicians assigned to the requests through business rules or category selection, will be overridden.

    Note:     While editing a request, Technician Auto Assign is applied to requests that remain unassigned even after the application of Business Rules and Category selection. The Auto Assign is not applied to requests that are assigned to technicians.

  5. You can choose to apply the Technician Auto Assign mechanism for only the technicians who are online by enabling Assign only to online technicians check box.

  6. You can also include or exclude certain technicians from the Technician Auto Assign functionality by enabling Include or Exclude following technicians toggle button. Select whether you want to Include or Exclude and select technicians by typing the technicians name in the given field.

  7. Enable the Rules toggle button to include or exclude certain requests from the Technician Auto Assign functionality. Choose Exclude/Include and start adding criteria to execute the rule. You can add multiple criterions and either make all criteria mandatory or only a few by choosing the"or" / "and" options.

  8. Click Save to save the configuration.
