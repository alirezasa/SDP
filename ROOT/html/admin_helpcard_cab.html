The Change Advisory Board (CAB) comprises a group of people who assist the Change Manager in the assessment, prioritization, and scheduling of changes. The members of the CAB are selected based on their expertise and capability to assess change adequately from a business and technical point of view.

Use the Search icon to find your preferred change advisory board.

Use the Actions icon to edit or delete a change advisory board.

Click on a CAB name or the drop-down icon beside it to view its associated members.

Click View History to view edits made to the change advisory boards.

Adding CAB

  • Click New.
  • Specify a Name for the CAB.
  • Enter a brief Description about the CAB.
  • Choose the CAB members.
  • Click Save.
