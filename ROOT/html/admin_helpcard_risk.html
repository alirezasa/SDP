Most of the proposed changes/releases may involve some level of risk during implementation. In order to successfully implement a change or release, the users involved should be aware of these risks.

By default, the application provides predefined risk levels in the list view. The configured risk level is assigned to a new change or release request.

Choose to view Active or Inactive Risk Levels using the dropdown.

Use the Actions icon to edit or delete a risk level.

Use the Search icon to find your preferred risk level.

Click View History to view the edits made to risk levels.

Add New Risk Level

  • Click New.
  • Provide a Name for the risk level.
  • Provide a brief Description about the potential risks associated with this level.
  • Click Save.

To save and add another risk level, click Save and Add New. Click Cancel to discard and go back to the list view.
