On this page, you will add the geographic regions where your company's branches are located.

Click New Region and fill out the displayed form.
Add the heads of each region such as Regional Manager and Regional Incharge.
You can also edit or delete the already added regions in the Regions home page (List View).

Note:The above mentioned configuration can be done from ESM directory alone and will not be available under individual service desk instances. When you delete a Region, its associated Sites and their attributes, such as groups, SLAs, business rules, and holidays, will also be deleted.
