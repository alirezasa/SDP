Merging duplicated accounts

On this page, you can merge accounts with duplicate Login Name or Email Address in different domains.

Select the criteria to identify the duplication as Login Name or Email Address, enter the domain names (two domains/one domain and no domain labelled as None), and click Apply Filter.

The duplicate account pairs across the selected domains will be listed. Select the accounts to be merged and click Merge user(s).

Select the parent domain and click Merge user(s).

After a merge process is completed, you can view its summary in notification or in Community>>System Log Viewer.


Note:

  • You can initiate a merge process only when no other merge process is underway.
  • All SDAdmins will be notified when a merge process is completed.
  • Site association of user assets will remain unchanged when you merge users from different sites.
