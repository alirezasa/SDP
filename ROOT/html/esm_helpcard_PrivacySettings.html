Privacy regulations and guidelines, such as the General Data Protection Regulation, require businesses to protect their user's personal data from misuse. GDPR protects all types of personal data, including the user's name, social security number, insurance details, or racial or ethnic data.
ServiceDesk Plus lets you completely delete or anonymize all personally identifiable information (PII).

From this page, you can only enable the PII option to be displayed in individual service desk instances. Option to identify PII is available in Incident Additional Fields, Service Catalog, and Resource Questions under individual Service Desk instances.
