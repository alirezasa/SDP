Site24x7

Site24x7 offers AI-based IT monitoring, reporting, and alerting from the cloud. It offers agent less and agent-based monitoring solutions to test your websites, API endpoints, internet services, synthetic transactions, server, public & private cloud, network, VMware, and application performance. Integrating ServiceDesk Plus with Site24x7 gives your technicians the power to perform remedy actions from within the ticket.

Highlights of Site24x7 Integration

  • Create quick action menus to perform automated incident resolution using Site24x7 IT automation. . You can associate SDP Incident templates with your preferred Site24x7 IT automation actions like list file sizes, clear logs, Start/Stop services and processes.
  • Address frequent service requests regarding application crashes or low disk space, that are raised as requests in SDP by remotely executing quick automation actions associated with the incident template.
Site24x7 for ServiceDesk Plus is available on the cloud

Disabling Site24x7 Menu

To remove Site24x7 integration with SDP, select Admin > Integrations > Site24x7, then uncheck the "Enable Site24x7 menu" option.
