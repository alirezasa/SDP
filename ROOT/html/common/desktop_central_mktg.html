Configure ManageEngine Endpoint Central (formerly Desktop Central) for your AssetExplorer/ServiceDesk Plus and integrate your Endpoint Management and Help desk needs.

Endpoint Central helps system administrators automate almost every aspect of system administration from managing patches, silently deploying business software, remote troubleshooting, restricting unwanted software and USB devices from network and lot more.

Benefits of Desktop Management Plug-in

  1. Endpoint Security & Management within AssetExplorer/ServiceDesk Plus.
  2. Round the clock Automated Patch Management for Windows, Linux, Mac OS and third party.
  3. Software Deployment to silently install/uninstall business software on-demand.
  4. Advanced remote control for seamless troubleshooting.
  5. Powerful tools to instantly resolve help desk tickets.
  6. Audit ready and real time asset details.
  7. Configuration templates to simplify various aspects of system administration.

Three Simple Steps to configure

  1. Download and install Endpoint Central Plug-in.
  2. Endpoint Central Settings in AssetExplorer/ServiceDesk Plus.
  3. Configure AssetExplorer/ServiceDesk Plus settings in Endpoint Central.

Disabling Endpoint Central Menu

If you are not interested in integrating the Desktop Management functions and wish to remove it, select Admin Endpoint Central (formerly Desktop Central), uncheck the “Enable UEM Menu” option.
