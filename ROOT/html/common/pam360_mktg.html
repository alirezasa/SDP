Integrate PAM360 with your ServiceDesk Plus

PAM360 provides a comprehensive solution for managing and monitoring privileged access to critical assets in your organization. It aims at safeguarding critical assets, ensuring compliance, and enhancing overall security.

PAM360 integration allows technicians to access privileged accounts in target machines (or resources) remotely from within ServiceDesk Plus, without manually providing account credentials. This also saves the time spent in entering the credentials for each remote session.
