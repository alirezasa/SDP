Integrate ManageEngine ADManager Plus with your ServiceDesk Plus to get ADManager Plus functions.

ADManager Plus is a simple, easy-to-use Windows Active Directory Management and Reporting Solution that helps AD Administrators and Help Desk Technicians with their day-to-day activities. With a centralized and Intuitive web-based UI, the software handles a variety of complex tasks like Bulk Management of User accounts and other AD objects, delegates Role-based access to Help Desk Technicians, and generates an exhaustive list of AD Reports, some of which are an essential requirement to satisfy Compliance Audits. This Active Directory tool also offers mobile AD apps that empower you to perform important user management tasks right from your mobile devices.

Steps to Install

  1. Download and install the ADManager Plus
  2. Configure ADManager Plus Settings in ServiceDesk Plus.

Disabling ADManager Plus Menu

If you are not interested in integrating the ADManager Plus functions and wish to remove it, select Admin ADManager Plus, uncheck the "Enable ADManager Plus Menu for technician" option.
