Integrate Key Manager Plus with your ServiceDesk Plus

Key Manager Plus is a web-based SSL/TLS certificate and SSH key management solution from ManageEngine. Integrating Key Manager Plus with Service Desk Plus enables technicians to identify the expired SSL/TLS certificates in target endpoints, invoke certificate renewal, and deploy the newly issued certificates to the respective applications, directly without leaving their Service Desk Plus consoles. Furthermore, this integration enables IT administrators to continuously monitor the SSL/TLS cipher strength across the network. Technicians are alerted whenever vulnerabilities such as weak cipher suites are spotted, allowing them to take immediate remedial action.
