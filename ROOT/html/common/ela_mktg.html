Steps to Integrate EventLog Analyzer with ServiceDesk Plus

  • Go to Admin >> Apps & Add-ons >> Integrations >> ManageEngine.
  • On the ManageEngine SIEM card, click Settings.
  • Configure the fields and click Save.

To remove the EventLog Analyzer button for the header pane, go to Admin >> Apps & Add-ons >> Integrations >> ManageEngine. On the ManageEngine SIEM card, click Settings and uncheck Enable EventLog Analyzer.
